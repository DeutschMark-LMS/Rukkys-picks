Rukky's Campus Food Tips

How to eat good food without cooking in your hostel

1. Choose More Protein

Eating only plain carbohydrates can make you sleepy during classes. If you have ₦2,000, buy one scoop of rice, two pieces of meat or eggs, and sauce. It gives you long-lasting energy for reading.

2. Go Before the Afternoon Rush

Fried plantain and good chicken sell out quickly by 2:00 PM. Go to the cafeteria around 12:45 PM as soon as your class finishes so you get fresh food.

3. Always Save Money for Cold Water

Always keep at least ₦200 for cold water in your budget. The weather on campus can be very hot, and you will need drinking water after eating.
